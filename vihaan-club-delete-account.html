Data Safety & Privacy

Request Account & Data Deletion

App Name: Vihaan Officers' Club  |  Developer: S2 Dev Labs

Registered users of Vihaan Officers' Club can request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time without needing to open the mobile application.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email from your registered email address (@indianoil.in or linked personal email) to the Vihaan Club Administrator at vihaanclub@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: App Account Deletion Request - Vihaan Club PRPC.
  3. Include your Full Name, Registered Email ID, and Employee ID (if applicable) so the administrator can verify ownership of the account.

Submit Request via Email:

Click below to open your email client with a pre-filled deletion request, or copy the format below.

Email Administrator to Delete Account
To: vihaanclub@gmail.com Subject: Account Deletion Request - Vihaan Club PRPC Full Name: [Your Full Name] Registered Email: [Your Registered Email] Employee ID (if applicable): [Your Employee ID]

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted Data: Your authentication credentials, user profile (name, primary and alternate email addresses, phone number, employee ID), linked family member sub-accounts, active facility subscriptions, and digital QR gate pass tokens are permanently deleted within 15 working days of verifying your request.
  • Retained Data (Audit & Accounting Compliance): Historical UPI payment transaction reference IDs and completed room/event booking invoices are retained in anonymized or minimal accounting form for up to 7 years solely to comply with internal club financial audit requirements.
