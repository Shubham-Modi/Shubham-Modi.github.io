Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Vihaan Officers' Club

Effective Date: November 22, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Vihaan Officers' Club ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Vihaan Club mobile application and web portal (collectively, the "Service") for the registered members of Vihaan Club at Panipat Refinery Township, Panipat, Haryana, India.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you use our Android application and web platform, in compliance with applicable Indian data protection laws and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to verify eligibility, manage club operations, and provide digital passes:

  • Account & Identity Information: Full name, email address (@indianoil.in for registered user verification), and assigned user role (Admin, Member, Non-Member, Family Member, or Staff).
  • Family Linkage Data: When a verified Club Member creates credentials for a family member, we store the association between the primary member account and the linked family member account.
  • Bookings, Events & Facility Records: Room reservation details, activity schedules, event and programme registrations, facility subscription dates, and digital QR pass tokens.
  • Gate Entry Logs: Entry timestamps and verification logs generated when security staff scan your digital QR pass at club facilities or event venues.

2. Device Permissions We Request

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly by authorized club security personnel (Staff role) to scan member and event QR codes at facility entrances. No photographs, videos, or facial biometrics are captured, stored, or transmitted to our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for internal club administration and member services:

  • Verify employee eligibility via @indianoil.in domain validation and manage role-based permissions.
  • Provision and manage separate login access for linked family members.
  • Process room bookings, recurring instructor reservations, event registrations, and facility passes.
  • Generate and validate unique QR codes for contactless access to club facilities and events.
  • Send automated booking confirmations, approval updates, and administrative notices via email.
  • Maintain security audit logs and prevent unauthorized access or misuse of club resources.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data, nor do we serve third-party advertisements. Data is shared strictly with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate the Service:

  • Supabase: Used for encrypted user authentication, PostgreSQL database hosting, and serverless backend functions.
  • Cloudflare: Used to host and deliver the web application securely over HTTPS.
  • Email Delivery Service (SMTP) through Gmail: Used to dispatch account verification links and confirmed booking notifications to users and club event schedulers.

5. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the Vihaan Club app and our backend servers is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS. Database access is governed by strict PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only view or modify records permitted by their assigned role.

We retain your profile, booking history, and transaction records for as long as your club account remains active or as required for internal club accounting and audit purposes.

6. Account Deletion & Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal data:

  • Profile Updates: You can update your alternate personal email address directly within the Club Hub tab of the application.
  • Account & Data Deletion: To request permanent deletion of your Vihaan Club account and associated personal data, please contact the Vihaan Club Administrator via email using the contact details below. Upon verification, your account credentials and personal profile data will be permanently deleted within 15 working days, except where minimal financial transaction records must be retained for club audit compliance.

7. Children's Privacy

The Vihaan Club app does not allow children under the age of 13 to self-register. Family member accounts for dependents are provisioned, controlled, and managed solely by verified adult members.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new club features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
